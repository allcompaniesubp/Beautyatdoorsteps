# BeautyDoorstep — Draft Customer Terms of Use

**Status: working draft for legal review. Not approved for launch.** These terms are a product-planning template, not legal advice. Qualified counsel must adapt them to each country and state/province before publication. Mandatory consumer, privacy, accessibility and professional rules override inconsistent wording.

**Draft version:** 0.1  
**Effective date:** To be set before launch  
**Platform operator:** The legal entity that owns and operates BeautyDoorstep must be identified here before launch. Do not assume the brand name itself is the contracting entity.

## 1. About the platform

BeautyDoorstep is intended to provide a directory and marketplace through which users may discover businesses, products, professionals, public information and services. Depending on location and launch status, the platform may support enquiries, quotes, bookings, orders or links to third-party websites.

BeautyDoorstep is not a government department, hospital, law firm, transport authority, bank, insurer, seller or service professional merely because it lists or connects users to one. A listing does not mean the platform endorses, employs, insures or guarantees that business or professional.

Some categories may be visible for discovery while transactions are unavailable. A service is available only when the platform expressly enables it for the relevant location and date.

## 2. Eligibility and accounts

You must meet the minimum legal age and capacity requirements in your jurisdiction. A parent or legally authorised guardian must act where the law requires it. Provide accurate, current information, protect your sign-in credentials and promptly report suspected account misuse. You are responsible for activity carried out through your account except where applicable law provides otherwise.

We may use proportionate security checks, suspend access during an investigation, or close accounts for fraud, unlawful activity, safety risks or repeated violations. We will provide notices and appeal routes where required by law.

## 3. Directory listings and third parties

Business and professional listings may be supplied by the business, public sources or authorised partners. Information can become outdated. Users should independently confirm identity, availability, price, qualifications, licence status, address and service scope before relying on a listing.

A third-party business is responsible for its own services, products, representations, contracts, warranties, tax obligations and legal compliance. Unless a checkout or contract expressly says otherwise, the contract for a service or product is between the user and the relevant provider or seller.

We will provide a process to report inaccurate, fraudulent, impersonating, unlawful or unsafe listings. We may verify, edit, restrict or remove listings under our moderation rules.

## 4. Quotes, bookings, orders and payments

Where enabled, displayed prices may be estimates and may exclude taxes, delivery, travel, materials, surcharges or other disclosed charges. The final price and scope must be confirmed before the user commits.

A request for a quote is not an accepted booking. A booking or order is confirmed only when the platform or provider sends an explicit confirmation. Payment methods, payment timing, refunds and payout arrangements will be shown at checkout or in the applicable policy.

Do not pay outside the platform where the transaction is represented as platform-protected. Conversely, do not assume an off-platform transaction is covered by platform payment protection. We will not claim to hold escrow or provide insurance unless the relevant service is legally established and expressly disclosed.

## 5. Cancellation, refunds and disputes

Cancellation windows, provider no-shows, late arrivals, returns, refunds, service rework and dispute steps must be displayed before purchase and governed by a separate country-specific policy. Nothing in these terms removes mandatory statutory refund, warranty or consumer rights.

Users should report safety issues, non-delivery, damage, suspected fraud or material service failures promptly and provide relevant evidence. We may facilitate communication but do not replace a court, regulator, insurer, payment provider or professional complaints body.

## 6. Lawyers and legal information

The platform is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice, legal representation or a lawyer-client relationship. General legal information and links are not a substitute for advice on an individual's facts.

A lawyer or advocate is solely responsible for professional eligibility, conflicts checks, advice, confidentiality, engagement terms, fees, filings and conduct. A user must independently confirm the professional's current authorisation and agree a written engagement directly with them.

Legal-professional discovery, advertising, rankings, lead generation, paid placement and referral arrangements will be enabled only after review under the applicable professional-conduct rules. In India, advocate advertising and solicitation restrictions require particular attention. The platform does not guarantee that every form of directory listing or contact workflow is permitted.

## 7. Doctors, healthcare and telemedicine

BeautyDoorstep is not an emergency medical service, hospital or substitute for clinical judgement. Healthcare services are available only where the relevant practitioner and facility are authorised and the platform has enabled the service.

The practitioner is responsible for deciding whether remote consultation is appropriate, identifying the patient and practitioner, obtaining required consent, maintaining clinical records, giving advice and prescriptions only when permitted, and arranging in-person assessment or emergency referral when necessary.

Do not use the platform for a medical emergency. Contact the local emergency number or attend an emergency department. Do not delay urgent care while using a directory or messaging feature.

Health information is sensitive. Any future health feature must have a separate privacy notice, appropriate consent, restricted access, security controls and legally compliant retention. Do not upload medical records into a public listing or ordinary support message.

## 8. Government and public-service information

Government-service pages are intended to help users find public information and genuine official portals. BeautyDoorstep is not a government authority and cannot approve benefits, issue official certificates, guarantee an application outcome or collect a government fee unless a specific authorised arrangement is clearly disclosed.

Users should check the agency, jurisdiction, official domain, fees, deadlines and requirements on the relevant official website. Where an official transaction is not integrated through a documented and authorised API, the user will be directed to the official portal. We will not request passwords, one-time passcodes or unnecessary identity documents for directory access.

## 9. Products and prohibited activity

Users and sellers must comply with product safety, consumer, import/export, intellectual-property, labelling and licensing rules. Counterfeit, stolen, recalled, unsafe, unlawful or restricted goods are prohibited. Additional category rules may apply to medicines, alcohol, tobacco, weapons, financial products, animals, hazardous materials and age-restricted goods.

No user may use the platform for fraud, harassment, discrimination, trafficking, exploitation, unlawful surveillance, money laundering, cyber abuse or other unlawful conduct.

## 10. Reviews, content and intellectual property

Users must submit honest, relevant reviews based on genuine experience. Fake reviews, review manipulation, threats, personal data disclosure, defamatory content and undisclosed conflicts are prohibited.

You retain ownership of content you submit. You grant the platform a limited, non-exclusive licence to host, display, format and moderate that content for operating and improving the service, subject to the privacy notice and applicable law. Do not upload content you do not have rights to use.

The BeautyDoorstep name, software, visual identity and original platform content are protected by applicable intellectual-property laws. No copying, scraping, reverse engineering or unauthorised commercial reuse is permitted except where law allows.

## 11. Location, communications and safety

Precise location must be requested only when needed for a user-selected feature, with clear purpose and consent. Location sharing for an active booking or trip must stop automatically when that activity ends, subject to a clearly disclosed, lawful record-retention need.

We may send essential account, booking, safety and transaction notices. Marketing communications require the consent or other lawful basis required in the relevant jurisdiction and must include an effective opt-out.

Users should meet providers safely, verify identity, keep communications and payment records, and report threats or incidents immediately. The platform cannot guarantee the conduct of every third party.

## 12. Privacy and security

Personal information is handled under the applicable Privacy Notice and local data-protection law. We aim to collect only what is necessary, restrict access, secure private documents, apply retention limits and provide legally required access, correction, deletion and complaint rights.

Never send passwords, one-time passcodes, full payment-card details or unnecessary government identity numbers to support staff.

## 13. Availability and service changes

The platform may be unavailable due to maintenance, connectivity, third-party outages, safety events or legal restrictions. Categories, providers, prices and features can vary by location and may be suspended. We will not describe planned features as live services.

## 14. Liability and consumer rights

To the extent permitted by law, the platform is not responsible for independent providers' professional decisions, products, acts or omissions. This does not exclude liability that cannot lawfully be excluded, including applicable consumer rights, fraud, wilful misconduct or other non-excludable duties.

Any limitation of liability, indemnity, warranty disclaimer or dispute clause must be tailored by local counsel and must not remove mandatory rights.

## 15. Complaints and contact

Before launch, publish the operator's legal name, registered address, customer-support contact, privacy contact and any locally required grievance officer details. Complaints should receive a reference number, acknowledgement and a response within the period required by applicable law.

## 16. Changes, governing law and severability

We will publish material changes and the date they take effect. Continued use after an effective date may constitute acceptance only where permitted by law and after any required notice or consent.

The governing law, courts, arbitration, language and dispute-resolution process must be specified in a jurisdiction-specific schedule after legal review. If a clause is unenforceable, the remaining clauses continue only to the extent allowed by law.
