# BeautyDoorstep — Draft Provider, Merchant and Business Listing Terms

**Status: draft for qualified legal review. Not approved for launch.** This template must be adapted for the operator and each jurisdiction. It does not create an employment, agency, partnership, franchise or professional relationship.

## 1. Who may join

A business, sole trader, merchant or professional may apply only if legally entitled to offer the listed goods or services in the relevant location. Applicants must provide accurate identity, ownership/authority, business, tax, bank/payout and professional information as lawfully required. Do not submit another person's credentials or documents.

## 2. Verification and approval

An application may remain pending until all checks are complete. Statuses are draft, pending, rejected, expired and approved. Approval is limited to the checks specifically described and does not guarantee service quality or replace a government licence, regulator registration, insurance or professional standing. Providers must promptly report changed, suspended or expired credentials.

BeautyDoorstep may request proportionate supporting evidence, conduct permitted checks, restrict a listing, pause transactions or remove an account for safety, fraud, legal or policy reasons. Providers may use the published review or appeal process where available.

## 3. Accurate listings and business authority

You warrant that you own or are authorised to manage the business listing, product content, photographs, trademarks, contact details and service descriptions. Keep pricing, stock, opening hours, service area, availability, qualifications and terms accurate. Do not create duplicate, misleading, impersonating or unauthorised listings.

The platform may label a listing as unverified, pending, sponsored or unavailable where appropriate. Do not claim official government affiliation, platform endorsement, professional certification or insurance unless the exact claim is true and substantiated.

## 4. Independent business and legal responsibility

You are an independent provider or seller, not an employee, agent or partner of BeautyDoorstep. You are responsible for your staff, subcontractors, licences, permits, tax, wages, workplace safety, insurance, product quality, consumer disclosures, warranties, fulfilment and compliance with all applicable laws.

You must not subcontract a regulated task to an unqualified person. You must obtain customer consent before entering premises, collecting information, taking photographs or using location data.

## 5. Professional and regulated services

For legal, healthcare, pharmacy, finance, insurance, transport, real estate, construction, electrical, security, childcare, veterinary and other regulated categories:
- Maintain all required registrations, licences, insurance and professional standing.
- Work only within your authorised scope and jurisdiction.
- Provide clear fees, risks, exclusions, complaint routes and written engagement/consent documents.
- Protect confidential, health, legal, financial and identity information.
- Follow professional advertising, referral, solicitation and conflicts rules.
- Do not use BeautyDoorstep to make misleading outcome, cure, legal-result, income or government-benefit guarantees.

Lawyers/advocates must comply with professional conduct rules, including restrictions on advertising and solicitation. Healthcare providers remain responsible for clinical judgement, patient identification, consent, records, prescriptions and emergency referral. The platform may disable these categories until a separate compliance approval is completed.

## 6. Products, stock and fulfilment

Merchants must sell lawful, genuine, safe and accurately described products. Maintain required product approvals, labels, invoices, warranties, recall processes, storage and delivery conditions. Counterfeit, stolen, unsafe, recalled, prohibited or restricted products are not allowed.

Keep stock and delivery promises current. Do not accept an order you cannot fulfil. Disclose total price, tax, shipping, delivery time, returns and warranty conditions before the buyer commits.

## 7. Enquiries, bookings, orders and service levels

Respond to customer requests within stated service windows. Confirm scope, location, price, timing, materials, exclusions and cancellation terms before accepting work. Do not mark work complete before it is complete. Keep proof of delivery or completion only where appropriate and lawful.

The platform may facilitate discovery, enquiries or transactions depending on the category and location. A listing or enquiry is not a guaranteed job or contract. The provider and customer must clearly agree the service contract and scope.

## 8. Fees, payments, tax and refunds

Any platform commission, subscription, listing charge, payment processing fee, tax withholding, payout schedule or reserve must be separately disclosed and accepted before it applies. No fee or commission is implied by this draft.

Providers must issue legally required invoices and account for applicable taxes. Refunds, cancellations, chargebacks and customer remedies must follow the published policy and mandatory local law. Do not request off-platform payment for a transaction represented as platform-protected.

## 9. Customer safety, complaints and cooperation

Treat customers respectfully and without unlawful discrimination, harassment or retaliation. Follow safeguarding, occupational safety, infection control, vehicle safety and emergency procedures relevant to your service. Report accidents, threats, suspected abuse, data incidents and serious complaints promptly.

Cooperate with reasonable investigations and preserve relevant records. Do not intimidate a customer, manipulate a review or retaliate against a complaint.

## 10. Data, privacy and location

Use customer information only to respond to the enquiry or fulfil the specific order/booking. Do not sell, export, scrape, retain indefinitely or use customer data for unrelated marketing. Obtain separate consent where required.

Precise location may be used only for the active service purpose and must stop when the booking/trip ends, subject to limited lawful records. Never request passwords, OTPs or unnecessary government ID documents. Store any approved credential documents only through the platform's designated private upload process.

## 11. Reviews, ranking and platform integrity

Do not buy reviews, create fake accounts, suppress genuine criticism, manipulate rankings, misrepresent competitors or offer undisclosed incentives for ratings. Any paid placement must be clearly labelled and offered only where lawful. Regulated professionals must not participate in promotional arrangements prohibited by their professional rules.

## 12. Intellectual property and content licence

You retain ownership of content you provide and grant the platform a limited licence to host, reproduce, resize, display and moderate it for directory and marketplace operation. You warrant that the content does not infringe third-party rights and may be removed if a credible rights complaint is received.

## 13. Suspension, termination and appeals

The platform may temporarily suspend listings or transactions to investigate safety, fraud, credential expiry, legal requests or policy breaches. Where required, we will give notice and a proportionate appeal process. Immediate restrictions may be necessary to protect users or comply with law.

## 14. Indemnity, liability and dispute terms

Any indemnity, limitation of liability, governing law, jurisdiction, arbitration or dispute clause must be drafted by local counsel and must not exclude mandatory statutory rights or liabilities that cannot legally be limited. This template intentionally does not invent the operating entity, fee schedule or governing-law forum.
