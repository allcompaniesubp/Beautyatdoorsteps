## India: legal and healthcare controls

- Bar Council of India Rule 36 restricts advocates from advertising or soliciting work. Obtain a written opinion from Indian legal counsel on the exact directory, profile, ranking, enquiry and payment design before enabling lawyer discovery or consultation booking.
- India's Telemedicine Practice Guidelines require professional judgement by the registered medical practitioner, appropriate identification and consent, and other safeguards. Use the current official guidance and obtain healthcare counsel review before enabling consultations.
- Never advertise an online doctor as verified solely because an uploaded certificate was received. Check the appropriate professional register and renewal/disciplinary status where an authorised verification route exists.
- Do not store clinical records in ordinary business listing tables or expose them to marketplace support staff.

## India: government-service directory and integrations

Initial safe product scope:
1. Maintain a curated directory of official agency websites, public schemes, office contacts and official application pages.
2. Record the government body, jurisdiction, official domain, source URL, last-checked timestamp and responsible reviewer.
3. Link users to the official portal when an authorised integration is not available.
4. Clearly state that BeautyDoorstep is an independent directory and not a government website or agent.
5. Do not collect government application fees, passwords, OTPs or identity documents for a directory listing.

Potential integration route:
- Review API Setu and the specific API/provider terms.
- Apply as the relevant eligible organisation/requester or partner.
- Wait for approval and API credentials; never use another organisation's credentials.
- Build only against the approved API scope and published technical specification.
- Complete required testing, security review/audit and go-live approval.
- Implement consent, purpose limitation, audit logs, retention and incident response.
- Reconfirm access and API status periodically; do not assume all public services have APIs.

DigiLocker issuer/requester access is a partner process. BeautyDoorstep must not claim DigiLocker verification or access until its own application is accepted and a permitted integration has passed testing.

## Launch checklist per jurisdiction

- [ ] Legal entity and local operator identified.
- [ ] Written local legal/regulatory assessment completed.
- [ ] Provider and facility credentials independently checked through an appropriate source.
- [ ] Insurance and professional indemnity confirmed where required.
- [ ] User consent, notices and contract terms approved.
- [ ] Payment, tax, refund and payout flows approved.
- [ ] Safety, complaint, incident and emergency escalation tested.
- [ ] Data protection impact assessment and cross-border transfer review completed.
- [ ] Category enabled only for approved service areas and provider capacity.
- [ ] Admin can pause the category or individual provider immediately.
